# Decisions and limits

- **Scope:** FIFA World Cup 2022, StatsBomb competition `43`, season `106`; the first audited profile uses three knockout fixtures.
- **Identity:** event players and recipients are source-grounded. Freeze-frame actors remain anonymous.
- **Coordinates:** raw coordinates support replay. Any later attack-direction normalization is a separately tested derived field.
- **Coverage:** a visible frame means only the camera-observed region at the event instant. It does not establish who was off camera or movement before/after the event.
- **Model boundary:** model estimates rank inputs under stated assumptions. They do not establish intent, counterfactual outcomes, or causal tactical superiority.
- **Publication boundary:** all shareable graphics and clips must cite StatsBomb Open Data, source revision, event/model identifiers, and state that they are tactical illustrations rather than footage.
