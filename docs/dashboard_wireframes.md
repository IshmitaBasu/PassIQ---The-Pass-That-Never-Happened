## Courage versus Caution

Recorded player/team selectors feed a probability-versus-threat comparison, model-estimated frontier membership for reliably linked chosen passes, and a named-recipient observed-pass network. Completed and attempted-pass denominators remain separate.

## Game-State Lens

Leading/drawing/trailing and period filters show sample sizes, uncertainty, xT zones, and descriptive comparisons. It never states that scoreline caused a decision.

## Evidence and limits

Show train/test boundaries, coverage gaps, calibration, source/model/render manifests, and the observed/modelled/illustrative legend. Every page repeats the data and tactical-reconstruction limitation where relevant.
