# Initial storyboards

## A. Decision clip — required MVP

1. Title/source card: competition, fixture, match/event ID, and “data-based tactical illustration — not match footage.”
2. Static observed 360 freeze-frame and selected pass.
3. Timed highlights for visible teammate locations.
4. Estimated probability/xT/frontier overlays.
5. Limitations/source end card: estimates do not show outcomes.

Actors remain at their one observed freeze-frame location; only arrows and labels animate.

## B. Observed sequence — portfolio enhancement

Cut between chronologically distinct observed events in a recorded possession. Each scene names its source event. Any connecting arrow says “diagrammatic transition.”

## C. Player/team comparison — portfolio enhancement

Animate recorded-pass aggregates, model-estimated option summaries, sample sizes, and uncertainty. Never attach player names to anonymous 360 dots.
